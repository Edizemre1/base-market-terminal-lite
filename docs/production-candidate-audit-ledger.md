## Invariants retained

- Production remains untouched and transaction execution stays environment-gated.
- Missing market values remain unknown; no zero, canonical price, tradeability, safety, event, countdown, or candle is invented.
- Same-symbol assets remain contract-first; exact pools remain distinct while one token opportunity remains deduplicated.
- Canonical pricing, provider pricing, on-chain state, liquidity evidence, quote readiness, and transaction readiness remain separate layers.
- Real wallet connection, approval, signature, swap, and transaction submission are outside this audit and are not performed.

## Final evidence

The authoritative release evidence is maintained in PR #32 and binds the final immutable SHA to its Actions run, artifact IDs, staging activation, detached 60-minute soak, retention result, rollback chain, and production-invariance proof.
